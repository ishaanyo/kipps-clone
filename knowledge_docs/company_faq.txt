Company FAQ – Kipps Clone Demo

Q: What does your AI Voice Agent do?
A: It handles inbound and outbound phone calls, qualifies leads, books appointments, answers FAQs, and can transfer to a human when needed.

Q: How much does it cost?
A: Pricing starts at $99 per month for the Starter plan which includes 500 minutes. Conversation cost is typically 5–12 credits per minute depending on the voice and LLM used.

Q: Do you support multiple languages?
A: Yes, we support 50+ languages including Hindi, English, Spanish, Arabic, and major Indian regional languages.

Q: Can it integrate with my CRM?
A: Yes. We have native integrations with HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, and can connect via webhooks or Zapier to almost any system.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. You can get free credits on signup and test the agent immediately with our "Call Me Now" feature.

Q: What industries do you serve?
A: EdTech, Healthcare, Hospitality, Real Estate, Financial Services, E-commerce, and more.

Q: How natural does the voice sound?
A: We use advanced TTS engines like ElevenLabs and Cartesia so the agent sounds human-like with natural pauses and intonation.

Q: Can the agent make outbound calls?
A: Yes. You can upload a lead list and the agent will automatically dial, qualify, and book meetings.
